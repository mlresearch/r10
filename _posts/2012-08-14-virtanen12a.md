abstract: Multi-modal data collections, such as corpora of paired images and text
  snippets, require analysis methods beyond single-view component and topic models.
  For continuous observations the current dominant approach is based on extensions
  of canonical correlation analysis, factorizing the variation into components shared
  by the different modalities and those private to each of them. For count data, multiple
  variants of topic models attempting to tie the modalities together have been presented.
  All of these, however, lack the ability to learn components private to one modality,
  and consequently will try to force dependencies even between minimally correlating
  modalities. In this work we combine the two approaches by presenting a novel HDP-based
  topic model that automatically learns both shared and private topics. The model
  is shown to be especially useful for querying the contents of one domain given samples
  of the other.
