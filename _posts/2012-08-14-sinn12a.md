abstract: As a new method for detecting change-points in high-resolution time series,
  we apply Maximum Mean Discrepancy to the distributions of ordinal patterns in different
  parts of a time series. The main advantage of this approach is its computational
  simplicity and robustness with respect to (non-linear) monotonic transformations,
  which makes it particularly well-suited for the analysis of long biophysical time
  series where the exact calibration of measurement devices is unknown or varies with
  time. We establish consistency of the method and evaluate its performance in simulation
  studies. Furthermore, we demonstrate the application to the analysis of electroencephalography
  (EEG) and electrocardiography (ECG) recordings.
