abstract: We consider a Markov decision process with deterministic state transition
  dynamics, adversarially generated rewards that change arbitrarily from round to
  round, and a bandit feedback model in which the decision maker only observes the
  rewards it receives. In this setting, we present a novel and efficient online decision
  making algorithm named MarcoPolo. Under mild assumptions on the structure of the
  transition dynamics, we prove that MarcoPolo enjoys a regret of O(T^(3/4)sqrt(log(T)))
  against the best deterministic policy in hindsight. Specifically, our analysis does
  not rely on the stringent unichain assumption, which dominates much of the previous
  work on this topic.
