abstract: 'We introduce a new cluster-cumulant expansion (CCE) based on the fixed
  points of iterative belief propagation (IBP). This expansion is similar in spirit
  to the loop-series (LS) recently introduced in [1]. However, in contrast to the
  latter, the CCE enjoys the following important qualities: 1) it is defined for arbitrary
  state spaces 2) it is easily extended to fixed points of generalized belief propagation
  (GBP), 3) disconnected groups of variables will not contribute to the CCE and 4)
  the accuracy of the expansion empirically improves upon that of the LS. The CCE
  is based on the same M{ö}bius transform as the Kikuchi approximation, but unlike
  GBP does not require storing the beliefs of the GBP-clusters nor does it suffer
  from convergence issues during belief updating.'
