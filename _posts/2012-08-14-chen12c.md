abstract: We create a formal framework for the design of informative securities in
  prediction markets. These securities allow a market organizer to infer the likelihood
  of events of interest as well as if he knew all of the traders’ private signals.
  We consider the design of markets that are always informative, markets that are
  informative for a particular signal structure of the participants, and informative
  markets constructed from a restricted selection of securities. We find that to achieve
  informativeness, it can be necessary to allow participants to express information
  that may not be directly of interest to the market organizer, and that understanding
  the participants’ signal structure is important for designing informative prediction
  markets.
